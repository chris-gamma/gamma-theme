# Changelog

Location Gamma's storefront theme is based on Shopify Horizon 4.4.0.

## Unreleased

### Added

- English/French storefront and editor translations, localized navigation and configurable three-level footer links.
- Gamma logos, Inter/Oswald typography, centered desktop navigation and responsive product titles.
- Gamma Red primary buttons and sale badges using native theme colour settings.
- Configurable product-information accordions with variant/product fallback, Gamma Red dividers and responsive specification tables.
- Failed product-selection recovery that restores the confirmed variant and cancels Add to Cart actions queued against the failed choice.
- Battery-system identification below the product price with localized icons, translated system types and collection links, plus native complementary accessory recommendations.
- Language-specific product media: images whose filenames end in -en/_en or -fr/_fr appear only in the matching language across galleries, zoom, cards, swatches, search, sticky and cart images, falling back to all media when a product has none for the current language.
- Full native controls for product videos, contained video presentation in the product gallery, and privacy-enhanced YouTube and Vimeo do-not-track embeds.
- Backorder and special-order notices before purchase controls, per-line cart availability text and evidence-based product-card badges, with native per-location pickup enabled.
- Open order notes in the cart, Shopify cart-line status messages, and a cart notice explaining supplier-dependent shipping for backordered or special-order items and how orders mixing in-stock items ship together; Shop Pay payment options are hidden in the cart and mobile Quick Add is enabled.
- Bilingual CAD formatting across product, quantity/volume, quick-order, cart, filter and gift-card displays, retaining native formatting for other currencies.
- Native Shopify privacy controls styled with the theme's typography, corners, wrapping and keyboard focus, retaining all consent choices and preference reopening.
- Brand, product-type, power-source, brand/type and brand/power-source collection templates. Their filters omit the brand, product-type or power-source choice the collection already fixes, through new Filters block settings, while a link that applies one keeps it visible and removable.
- A home search field beneath the hero slogan. Typing opens the header's search dialog with the same query, suggestions and keyboard navigation; it remains an ordinary search form, with a submit button, when that dialog isn't ready or JavaScript is off. The field and the instruction below it, which tells customers suggestions may open as they type, sit on a translucent dark background over the hero image. Width, corners, border, background colour and opacity, text colour and text size are editable in the theme editor.
- Rental equipment in search, set up in Theme settings > Search with separate French and English rental site addresses (enabled with the current rental site; off by default for themes without these settings). The search dialog shows up to four matching items from the rental site's catalogue above the store's own results, as cards with uncropped photos opening each item's rental page in a new tab, and a link beside the heading that opens all rental results in a new tab. With rental search on, the dialog grows to use more of a tall desktop window. A switch beside the heading turns rental results off for the rest of the browser tab's visit: the catalogue is no longer searched, and the heading keeps a link to see rental results on the rental site. The group stays hidden when nothing matches, replaces the store's “no results” message when only rentals match, and offers a rental site search link if the catalogue can't be reached. The search results page links to the same rental search, without needing JavaScript.
- A “Who we are” introduction below the featured products, set in a wider column on larger screens. The featured products are headed as a section of the page.
- A four-part footer: the logo, newsletter sign-up, Facebook and Instagram links and accepted payment methods; the store's address, telephone and email; and the shop, company, legal and account menus, titled by their menu names. The powered-by notice is off and the terms and policies list is unchanged.
- A contact page with the store's contact details, then the page's own content and the contact form. Department cards written in the page content as a display-card-table of display-card boxes form a grid that wraps on narrow screens; their styles load only on pages that use them.
- A Policy block that shows a selected warranty or return-and-exchange entry with the same text product pages show, as a section or as a collapsible row; collapsible Policy blocks one after another form a single list. A warranty page template (warranty-info) lists the used-equipment warranty as a collapsible row below the page content. A comparison page template (leaf-blower-chart) keeps pages assigned to it working.
- Store hours in the footer (compact) and on the contact page (full), chosen once in Theme settings > Store hours: an override, otherwise the dated exception in effect, otherwise the regular hours, from Store hours entries. Exceptions show their holiday notice above a seven-day table, highlighted days are bold Gamma Red with a bar and screen-reader text, and narrow columns stack each day above its hours. Without trustworthy hours, customers see a message asking them to contact the store; the theme editor explains why.
- A Map block in the footer and beside the contact page's business details: a static map image with a linked © OpenStreetMap credit, and Google Maps and Apple Maps directions built from the block's address, all working without JavaScript. Clicking the image, or pressing Enter or Space on it, loads the block's uMap map in place, with only zoom and full-screen controls and its credits under uMap's ? button, a loading indicator, and a close button in the corner that turns it off; a failure message and Try again appear after 20 seconds or when offline. Without an image, a “Show interactive map” button loads it. Turning maps on or off applies to every map and is remembered on the device; without a choice, maps load on their own as they come into view when Shopify's customer privacy settings allow preferences, and turn off again if that permission is withdrawn. The uMap map ID, address, images, aspect ratios, widths and a maximum width of up to 480 px are block settings; the contact page shows a 4:3 map up to 480 px wide and the footer a square map up to 180 px. The static image is drawn at the map's largest size and centred, so a narrower box crops it as it crops the interactive map, and `scripts/generate-map-previews.mjs` generates matching previews from the theme's settings.
- Promotion pages from Shopify campaign content in French and English: a campaign page with its offers, how each is redeemed (including dealer-supplied gifts, which are never added to the cart), qualifying products as native cards with Quick Add, or, when only some models qualify, cards linking to the eligible model (at most 60 per page) and rental equipment cards; notices instead of offers when a promotion is scheduled, paused, ended or unavailable, with manufacturer claim instructions kept after the sale until their deadline; a promotions index sorted by priority and end date, with sponsor badges, paged 24 at a time with every promotion listed without JavaScript; and a callout on collection pages for their current promotions. Sponsor badges on index cards, campaign pages and collection callouts show the sponsor's name and colour from its Brand entry in Shopify content, or the theme's brand colour for Location Gamma, with black or white text for contrast, and a plain outlined badge when no colour is set. Time-limited content disappears from open pages when it ends.

### Changed

- French contact and newsletter forms say “Courriel” instead of “E-mail”, and the password page sign-up button reads “Subscribe”. Long email addresses in text wrap instead of widening narrow layouts.
- French backorders read “En rupture de stock” (“En rupture” on product-card badges) instead of “Épuisé”, which is reserved for sold-out items.
- Promotion pages follow the first stage of the storefront's new design language: a 56 px campaign title (36 px on phones), smaller offer and group headings, a larger opening summary, and terms as quiet fine print under small labels instead of headings louder than the offers. Redemption and gift explanations share one notice style, with the gift icon in Gamma Red beside its lead-in. A gift is named once, with a linked picture of the gift product when one is set; cards for specific eligible models show the theme's “Sold out”, backorder and special-order badges and name the eligible models only when a product has several; a limited gift offer reads correctly for a single qualifying order; and a rental group with no equipment to show is left out.

- Updated the Horizon foundation from 4.3.0 to 4.4.0, retaining Gamma's customizations and English/French locales. Primary buttons now use the Accent font's weight, as configured.

### Fixed

- Adding to cart, Quick Add and discount changes keep the cart in the shopper's language instead of re-rendering English carts in French.
- The contact form's phone field accepts ordinary formats such as (514) 481-5631, 514 481 5631 and +1 514 481 5631 (fix for Horizon 4.4.0 in `blocks/contact-form.liquid`; remove once Horizon stops limiting the field to digits and hyphens).
- Back and Forward after changing collection or search filters or sorting now update the results, filters and counts instead of leaving the later results on screen (workaround for Horizon 4.4.0; see `assets/gamma-facets-history.js`).
- A search query typed while predictive search is still loading recently viewed products is no longer erased about 100 ms later (fix for Horizon 4.4.0 in `assets/predictive-search.js`; remove once Horizon stops clearing a typed query when it loads the empty state).
- Pressing Enter in the search dialog opens the highlighted suggestion or result, even when the search has exactly one product, collection, page or article result (fix for Horizon 4.4.0 in `assets/predictive-search.js`).
